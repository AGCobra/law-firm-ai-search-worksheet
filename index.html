Use the worksheet

Keep the evidence
with the observation.

01

Run a consistent sample

Save the exact questions before you begin. Record your firm's name and website, the AI search tool, its search mode, the date and time, and relevant location or account settings. Run each question separately. Keep the first result you observe, including an empty result or an error, so the record reflects the sample you planned.

02

Record what appeared

Use the recorder or your own observation sheet to save the answer and source URLs. Mark a firm mention separately from a link to your firm's website. Use yes, no, or unclear only after checking; leave untested results blank. Verify descriptions against facts your firm can substantiate and record the exact claim when you find an error.

03

Compare later observations

Import your saved runs backup and select a run. Choose “Use these questions for a new run” to retain its exact questions, then enter the new search date and context. Record fresh observations and note website changes between runs. A missing result does not establish index absence, and seven questions cannot describe every prospective client's experience.

Before you compare

Your manual audit
checklist.

  • Confirm the practice area and location match the firm's actual services.
  • Save the fixed query list and identify the firm and website being reviewed.
  • Record the tool, search mode, relevant settings, and date and time.
  • Run each question separately and save the answer, sources, or error.
  • Distinguish a name mention from a linked website citation.
  • Check factual descriptions against information your firm has approved.
  • Choose specific accuracy, content, or website issues to investigate.
  • Repeat the same sample later and retain both sets of observations.

A record, not an automatic score

You enter each observation after checking the response. The recorder counts only the responses you mark reviewed. It does not contact search engines, assess visibility automatically, or promise a ranking. A website link, a firm mention, and an accurate description answer different questions.

Common questions

Read results in context.

Does this test my firm automatically?

No. It creates seven questions and records your manual entries. You run the searches in the tool you choose, review the answers and source links, and enter what appeared. It does not generate legal answers, check legal citations, or recommend providers.

Does a missing result mean we are not indexed?

No. These questions show what appeared in specific searches. They do not expose an index inventory or explain why a page was omitted. Record the absence without assigning a cause the evidence does not establish.

What can we compare between runs?

Compare firm mentions, website links, and description accuracy for the same questions. Start another run for a later session; earlier runs keep their questions and context. Download all runs to retain the dates, settings, and observations beyond this visit. The recorder does not calculate a universal visibility score.
